# EDIZO PLATFORM - PREMIUM UI/UX DESIGN PROMPT

Design a world-class, modern SaaS platform called "Edizo" that combines a Software Development Agency, Internship Academy, Learning Platform, and Client Service Management System.

The design must feel premium, trustworthy, innovative, and future-ready. Avoid generic templates. The interface should look like a blend of Stripe, Linear, Framer, Notion, and Apple design principles while maintaining a unique Edizo identity.

## Brand Personality
* Modern
* Professional
* Educational
* Innovative
* Premium
* Trustworthy
* Future-Focused

## Color Palette
Primary:
* Vibrant Orange (#FF7A00)
* Soft Orange (#FF9F43)
Secondary:
* Modern Gray (#4B5563)
* Light Gray (#F5F7FA)
Accent:
* White (#FFFFFF)
* Warm Silver (#E5E7EB)
Success:
* Green (#10B981)
Warning:
* Amber (#F59E0B)
Error:
* Red (#EF4444)

Avoid dark-heavy UI.
Use bright white backgrounds with elegant orange accents.

## Typography
Headings:
* Poppins
* Sora
* Plus Jakarta Sans
Body:
* Inter

Use large typography and generous spacing.

## Design Style
* Glassmorphism Cards
* Soft Shadows
* Rounded Corners (16-24px)
* Floating Elements
* Smooth Gradients
* Micro Interactions
* Framer Motion Animations
* Modern Illustrations
* Animated Background Shapes
* Hover Effects
* Scroll-triggered Animations

## Layout Rules
* Fully Responsive
* Mobile First
* Tablet Optimized
* Desktop Premium Experience
* Consistent 12-column Grid
* Large White Space
* Card-based Architecture
